# jepa-mean-collapse

#Research Question

Does JEPA exhibit mean-prediction collapse, and under what conditions does it occur?

#Hypothesis 1 — Mean Prediction

When context representations provide insufficient information about target representations, an MSE-trained predictor approaches the conditional mean.

#Hypothesis 2 — Representation Learning

When encoders are jointly trained, representation collapse and predictor mean collapse may exhibit different behaviors.

#Experiments

* Exp01: Mean Prediction under Fixed Representations
* Exp02: Collapse Analysis with Learnable JEPA Encoders
